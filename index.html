Do not buy a bond, file an LLC, or set up the office until the exact address clears.

A wholesale only license can avoid a public display lot, but your actual home office still must be acceptable to the local jurisdiction and DMV. A wrong address, use description, or office setup can waste every later filing fee.

Your only hard stop today

Confirm the address and proposed use in writing with the jurisdiction that actually governs the parcel, then ask DMV Occupational Licensing how it will evaluate the specific home office. Do not assume a garage, sign, customer visit, vehicle storage, or a generic online checklist is acceptable.

LLC setup

Form the LLC with California, then connect it to the local jurisdiction and DMV.

There is no separate "Thousand Oaks LLC." California creates the LLC. The local jurisdiction then approves the business address and DMV separately licenses the wholesale dealer business.

Keep the order straight

Clear the exact address first. Then form the LLC with the California Secretary of State. Do not use a generated PDF for this step: the State currently lists both LLC formation and the initial Statement of Information as online only through bizfile. A local approval packet does not create an LLC, and an LLC does not authorize vehicle dealing.

1

Choose the exact name and responsible people

Before opening the State filing

Decide the exact legal name ending in LLC, who will manage it, and who will be the California agent for service of process. Use the same legal name, ownership names, and physical address everywhere after this point.

  • Use the California Business Search as a preliminary check, then use the exact chosen name in the State filing. The State can reserve a name for 60 days, but still reviews it when you file.
  • Use an address you are comfortable placing on a State business record. Do not use a P.O. Box where the State requires a street address.
  • If the dealership will use a different public name, plan the applicable fictitious business name filing before the DMV packet.

Check LLC name rules

2

Form the California LLC online

State step, not a City step

Use the Secretary of State bizfile portal to submit the Articles of Organization, Form LLC-1. The official California domestic LLC page lists formation as online only and shows a $70 filing fee. Save the accepted filing and the assigned California entity number.

3

File the Statement of Information

Required after formation

File the LLC Statement of Information, Form LLC-12, online within 90 days of initial registration. California lists the $20 filing fee and requires a new filing every two years. Save the completed State record because DMV asks for a current Statement of Information when the applicant is an LLC.

4

Get the EIN and set the tax calendar

Only after the State accepts the LLC

Apply for an EIN directly from the IRS after the LLC exists. The IRS EIN is free. Budget the known State costs separately: $70 to form the LLC, $20 for the initial LLC-12, and generally an $800 California annual LLC tax due by the 15th day of the fourth month after the beginning of the taxable year. Get tax advice if your ownership or projected income is complex.

The clear path

Work in this order after the address is approved.

Each phase depends on the one above it. This sequence is more reliable than trying to finish everything on a fixed 30 day calendar.

1

Lock the business identity

Only after address approval

Choose sole proprietor, LLC, partnership, or corporation. Decide the exact legal name, any DBA, every owner or manager who controls the business, and the physical and mailing addresses. Do not abbreviate or vary these details from form to form.

  • If using an LLC: follow the California LLC setup path, obtain the federal EIN after formation, and retain the current online Statement of Information.
  • If using a DBA: complete the applicable fictitious name filing before you submit the DMV packet. The DMV checklist requires it when applicable.
  • Open the business bank account: the DMV application and financial release need its details later. Do not put account numbers into this website.
2

Complete tax and local approvals

Collect proof, do not rely on verbal approval

Apply for the CDTFA seller permit and save the issued permit. For 1030 Calle Rey, use Ventura County's Home Occupation Zoning Clearance packet and County Business Tax Certificate application together after Planning confirms that the truthful operation qualifies. The County says a successful submission is stamped or emailed as the zoning clearance. Do not use a Thousand Oaks City home packet for this address. For another location, use the jurisdiction that governs that physical office.

  • Keep a PDF or screenshot of the seller permit, County application, stamped clearance or emailed certificate, and every condition.
  • Do not advertise vehicles, store business vehicles, or install a sign at the residence unless the local authority expressly allows it.
  • Use the dedicated office to keep records available during the business hours you provide to DMV.
3

Qualify the owners

Each owner must be handled separately

Complete a DMV approved dealer education program and follow DMV's current instructions for the used dealer written examination. Save the original certificate and proof of passing. The checklist says the certificate is valid for one year.

  • For each person listed under ownership, complete the personal history questionnaire, Live Scan process, and agent for service of process form.
  • Bring the official DMV 8016 form to the Live Scan site. The operator completes the transaction information and gives you the receipt copy for the packet.
  • Do not let this page answer personal history, employment, criminal history, date of birth, Social Security, driver license, or Live Scan fields for you.
4

Choose the correct security route

The projected first year volume changes the form

Tell the bond producer your exact legal entity name, DBA, and estimated annual wholesale volume. The name on the bond must match the DMV application.

Fewer than 25 vehicles

Wholesale only dealers projecting fewer than 25 vehicles in a year may use OL 25B, the $10,000 bond form, if they qualify.

25 or more vehicles

Use OL 25, the standard $50,000 dealer surety bond. The old plan incorrectly treated $10,000 as universal.

Deposit alternative

OL 25E plus STD 204 is an alternative security route. Use it only if you intentionally choose a deposit agreement.

5

Assemble, submit, then schedule inspection

Keep one complete copy before you submit

Use the OL 248B checklist in its listed order. Submit through the current DMV occupational licensing process and pay the applicable fees. DMV's current dealer page says the online application is processed before it sends instructions to schedule an inspection. Do not promise customers, auctions, or lenders a date until DMV confirms it.

  • Take current location and office photos only after the space matches what you will show the inspector.
  • Wholesale only dealers do not need a display area or DMV business sign, but they do need a legitimate business office and records.
  • After licensing, add insurance, auctions, transport, and financing in that order. Never sell to retail customers under a wholesale only license.

Your DMV packet

Use this exact checklist before you submit.

The official OL 248B checklist is the source of truth. Some documents are required once, some are required for every owner, and one security route must be chosen.

Core application order

  1. OL 248B Used Dealer or Dealer Wholesale Only Application Checklist.
  2. OL 12 Application for Original Occupational License.
  3. OL 21A Original Application for Occupational License. The old plan listed OL 16, which is not the correct form.
  4. One security route: OL 25, or qualifying OL 25B, or OL 25E plus STD 204.
  5. OL 53 Authorization to Release Financial Information.
  6. For every person under ownership: OL 29B, ADM 9050, and the DMV 8016 Live Scan receipt.
  7. Attachments: passing exam evidence, original dealer education certificate, current LLC Statement of Information if applicable, fictitious name statement if applicable, seller permit, and business location photos.

What to keep beside the packet

Local approval and conditions, a lease or ownership evidence if requested, office layout notes, photo originals, all receipts, and a copy of every signed form.

What this site will not prefill

Signatures, notarization, official DMV fields, surety fields, bank account information, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, driver license numbers, Live Scan information, or personal history disclosures.
